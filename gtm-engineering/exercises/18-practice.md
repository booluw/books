# Exercises: Chapter 18, Practice

## Questions

1. The CRO says "we need more enterprise pipeline". Write the first five questions you ask and the data you pull.
2. Write a QA checklist for launching a new outbound play.
3. Draft a weekly update for stakeholders.
4. Which anti-patterns from §18.7 have you seen? How would you fix one?

## Solutions

1. Questions: target and timeframe? Current enterprise funnel by stage (where's the gap)? Which segments win? Capacity (AEs, SDRs)? What's been tried? Data: last 12 months of enterprise opportunities by source, stage conversion, win rate by segment, inbound volume and speed-to-lead, outbound play metrics.
2. Sample rows reviewed; exclusions and suppression verified with test records; enrichment accuracy spot-checked; copy reviewed by a seller; AI lines validated; deliverability ready (DNS, warmup, caps); compliance (countries, LIA, opt-out); tracking and dashboard ready; kill criteria set.
3. Shipped; results (metrics vs targets); issues and incidents; next week's priorities; asks.
4. Personal.
