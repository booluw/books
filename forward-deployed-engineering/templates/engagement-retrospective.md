## How to use this template

- **Purpose:** learn from the engagement, honestly and without blame, so that the next one goes better: compare what was promised with what happened, find root causes, decide what should become product, and change the team's playbook.
- **When:** at the end of the engagement in Phase 7 (Expand and feed back) of the [engagement lifecycle](../chapters/04-engagement-lifecycle.md), and at major phase exits for long engagements (for example after the proof of value). Hold it within two weeks of the end, before memories fade.
- **Owner:** the lead FDE or the FDE manager facilitates; ideally someone not on the engagement for contentious ones.
- **Sign-off:** the FDE manager agrees the actions; product leadership agrees the "productise" list. Run two versions: one **with the customer** (sections 1–4 and 7, focused on the shared work) and one **internal** (all sections, including commercial and playbook points).

Background reading: [Chapter 4 §4.13](../chapters/04-engagement-lifecycle.md) and [Chapter 16](../chapters/16-field-to-product.md).

*Blameless means focusing on systems, decisions and information, not on individuals' failings. Ask "what made this the reasonable thing to do at the time?" rather than "who got this wrong?".*
