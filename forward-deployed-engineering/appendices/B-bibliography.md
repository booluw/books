# Appendix B. Bibliography and Sources

Every source cited in the chapters' "Further reading" sections and in the text, grouped by topic. Each entry names the chapter(s) that cite it.

## How to read this list

- **Currency.** Facts that change over time (hiring figures, company programmes, salaries, regulations, product names) were checked in **October 2026** and are marked *(as of October 2026)* in the chapters. Re-check them before relying on them.
- **URLs.** URLs are given only where they are well known and stable. Elsewhere the publication, publisher or site is named so you can search for the current location.
- **Source types.** Much public writing about forward deployed engineering comes from parties with an interest in the topic. Entries are flagged:
  - **[company]**: a company describing its own products, programmes or results (primary, but not neutral);
  - **[vendor]**: a seller of products, data or training related to the topic;
  - **[recruiter]**: a recruiting firm or job-posting aggregator;
  - **[investor]**: a venture capital firm writing about its market;
  - **[third-party]**: an independent guide, newsletter or self-reported data site, not affiliated with the companies it describes.
- **Standards and law** are primary sources. Where a regulation's timeline has been subject to change (notably the EU AI Act), check the current position with legal counsel.
